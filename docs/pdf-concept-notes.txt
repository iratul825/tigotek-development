THE BRIEF BEHIND THE SKETCH
“We are going to be the next Fiverr / Upwork.”
A marketplace HQ has three jobs: earn client trust at the door, give the partner network a home, and let the in-house
team ship fast. Every change on this page serves one of those three.
KEPT AND UPGRADED
Engineers x 2
Two squads, 24 desks. Clusters run at right angles to the glass so no screen
faces a window. A phone pod closes every cluster.
Technicians
Tech Ops suite: 6-seat NOC with video wall, tech bench, MDF comms room
and IT store. Glass to the corridor so escorted clients see the operation.
CEO, Director
Kept on the quiet top floor and right-sized (20 m² and 18 m²). Glazed fronts
with blinds, EA desk outside.
Conference on L2
Becomes the war room: 8 seats, whiteboard walls, beside the squads.
Studio, soundproof room, studio extras
One isolated Content Studio suite: cyc studio, podcast booth, shared control
room, green room, kit store, one controlled door.
Marketing, Conference on L1
Creative and media studio with 16 desks, a pin-up wall and a 6-seat review
room.
Thinking room, reading room
One library. Quiet, no calls, daylight on two sides.
Lobby, sitting place, pods, big ceiling
Arrival hall with host desk and two meeting pods. Ground floor set at 4.5 m
floor to floor.
Café, networking
Work café: coffee bar, 28 seats with power, partner lockers on the brand
wall.
MOVED
Stage: L1 to ground
Now the Forum: stage, LED wall, 3-tier step seating with chair storage
underneath. Events stay on the public floor and 100 guests leave at street level.
Client meetings: L2 to ground
Client suite at arrival: boardroom, meeting room, interview room. Visitors stop at
the security line unless escorted.
REMOVED
Waiting room on L2
It duplicates the ground floor lobby.
Tech lead's closed room
The lead sits with both squads. The war room covers ceremonies and
one-to-ones.
Separate thinking and reading rooms
One library does both jobs.
Stand-alone soundproof room
Absorbed into the studio suite.
ADDED: MISSING FROM THE SKETCH
Security
Host desk, speed gates and one security line in front of the lift and Stair 1.
Code and safety
Two protected stairs, a lift and an accessible WC on every floor.
Faith and wellbeing
Prayer rooms for men and women with wudu, plus a lockable wellness / parent
room.
Daily support
Tea point on L1, pantry on L2, electrical / IPS room, MDF comms room, IT
store.
Marketplace kit
Interview / vetting room for partner assessments, partner lockers, power at every
café seat.
